# Board Recruitment Packet (Preparatory Draft)

Version: 0.1.0
Status: preparatory material — the board is NOT seated and governance is NOT
active. Per [BOARD-STATUS](BOARD-STATUS.md), recruitment, seating, acceptance,
and ratification are human-owned external acts. This packet only prepares the
outreach material so those acts can begin; it creates no governance evidence.

## What this is

An invitation package for candidate members of a future independent board that
will steward a neutral, reproducible public comparison of AI memory systems
(see [CHARTER](CHARTER.md)). The operator of this repository builds one of the
candidate systems (Mnemosyne) and therefore holds a permanent, incurable
conflict for that system ([CONFLICT-OF-INTEREST](CONFLICT-OF-INTEREST.md));
the board exists precisely so that no number about any entrant — including
Mnemosyne — is published on the operator's own authority.

## Open seats (per charter §Roles and composition)

| Seat | Count | Profile |
|---|---|---|
| Independent academic methodologist | 2 | Evaluation methodology, benchmarking, or IR/NLP measurement background; no vendor affiliation with any entrant |
| Reproducibility lead | 1 | Experience running or auditing independent reproductions; comfort with pinned-artifact, digest-verified pipelines |
| Security/privacy lead | 1 | Adversarial evaluation, memory-poisoning/prompt-injection literature, or applied security review |
| Community representative | 1 | Practitioner perspective; represents benchmark consumers, not any vendor |

A non-voting operator liaison (the Mnemosyne operator) supplies records and
custody artifacts but holds no vote and no evidence-custody role for
contested matters. No vendor may hold a voting majority.

## What members do

- Approve methodology versions (quorum + two-thirds of eligible members).
- Adjudicate appeals and disputes ([APPEALS-AND-DISPUTES](APPEALS-AND-DISPUTES.md)).
- Publish corrections; authorize releases of results.
- Enforce the equal-treatment operator firewall
  ([OPERATOR-FIREWALL](OPERATOR-FIREWALL.md)) — the board may never waive it,
  conceal run history, silently alter scores, or grant private access.

Estimated commitment: an activation meeting, then low, bursty load —
methodology votes and appeal adjudications as they arise.

## What members get to inspect

Everything. The evaluation harness, scoring, custody ledgers, preregistration
records, attempt history (including failures), and reproduction bundles are
inspectable end to end. The project's own results remain unpublishable until
this board exists and ratifies policy — candidates should read that as the
central credibility mechanism, not a formality.

## Eligibility and independence

- No current employment, equity, or funded relationship with any entrant
  vendor (including the operator's).
- Conflicts must be disclosed publicly before deliberation; conflicted members
  recuse from discussion, evidence custody, and votes on that system.
- The operator's Mnemosyne conflict is permanent and cannot be cured by
  disclosure — voting seats therefore exclude the operator entirely.

## How acceptance works (human-owned steps)

1. Candidate reviews this packet plus [CHARTER](CHARTER.md),
   [METHODOLOGY](METHODOLOGY.md), and [CONFLICT-OF-INTEREST](CONFLICT-OF-INTEREST.md).
2. Written acceptance of the charter terms.
3. Signed public conflict disclosure (template in the COI register).
4. Chair selection among the seated independent members; role assignments.
5. External ratification of charter + methodology; every gate in
   [BOARD-STATUS](BOARD-STATUS.md) must hold verifiable public evidence before
   anything may be described as neutral or active.

## Candidate sourcing notes (for the operator)

Suggested pools, to be pursued by the operator personally: program-committee
members of reproducibility tracks (ML/IR venues), maintainers of established
public benchmarks/leaderboards, academic evaluation-methodology groups,
security researchers publishing on memory/RAG attacks, and practitioner
communities that consume memory-system benchmarks. Outreach, negotiation, and
seating are external human acts and must not be represented as performed or
scheduled by any agent.
