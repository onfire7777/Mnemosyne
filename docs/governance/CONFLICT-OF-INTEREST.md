# Conflict-of-Interest Policy

Version: 0.1.0  
Status: draft; External activation required

Mnemosyne is both a benchmark subject and the source repository's product.
That is a permanent conflict, not a conflict that disclosure, recusal, funding,
or organizational separation can eliminate. Its treatment is non-waivable.

All members, operators, judges, contributors, and funders disclose employment,
investment, grants, advisory roles, close collaboration, and recent advocacy.
Disclosures are public in [COI-REGISTER](COI-REGISTER.md) and updated before any
affected decision. Affected decision-makers recuse; minutes record the recusal.

Mnemosyne personnel may maintain the shared harness and submit ordinary public
artifacts, but may not validate Mnemosyne's score, handle its private appeal,
select favorable runs, or vote on its ranking. Independent operators must
reproduce any future headline-eligible Mnemosyne result.
