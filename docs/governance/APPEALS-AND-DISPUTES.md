# Appeals and Disputes

Version: 0.1.0  
Status: draft; External activation required

Any participant or observer may file an evidence-backed appeal against a run,
adapter, metric, eligibility decision, or policy application. The public appeal log
records receipt time, scope, evidence hashes, assigned reviewers, recusals,
interim status, decision, vote, and linked correction.

Two eligible reviewers triage within ten business days. Affected reviewers
declare recusal. The appellant receives the evidence and may respond. Final
decisions require quorum under the charter, a written rationale, and a public
record. Security-sensitive evidence may be redacted narrowly, with the reason
and hash of the sealed original recorded.

An appeal never edits history. If sustained, change control publishes a new
result version linked bidirectionally to the superseded record.
