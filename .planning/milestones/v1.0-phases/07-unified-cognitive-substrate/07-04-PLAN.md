<objective>
Let the mind earn broader autonomy where it has proven itself — automatically, with no human flipping anything — without ever letting it bootstrap its own delusion. Build the promotion law: per-domain credentials track the historical EXTERNAL corroboration outcome of the mind's own self-thoughts, and where the track record is strong, the BIRTH groundedness of new self-thoughts in that domain rises. Bound it with the Goodhart meta-rail (H11): external-corroboration-only credentials (R5), holdout validation, provenance-assigned domains, bounded uplift, decay on falling corroboration — and keep the permanent evidence-dominance gap (H3).

CRITICAL: this is the single highest-risk component. It ships ONLY if an adversarial echo-chamber/sleeper-poison corpus provably cannot raise a credential, the evidence-dominance gap holds, and no reliability guardrail regresses. A credential may move a self-thought UP WITHIN the self band; it may never cross into the external-evidence band.
</objective>
