### Fixture

- Path: `eval/public/fixtures/wmbs-m06-consolidation-development.json`.
- Families: repeated, corroborated, contradictory, procedural,
  related-transfer, and unrelated-control.
- Five cycles per case. At least five seeds.
- Byte-identical under `canonical_json(generate_fixture(seed))`.
- Events that claim conformance bind to the existing closed ABI
  `$defs.portable_event`. Do not edit the schema file.
- Synthetic deterministic local episodes only. No external dataset, no
  network, and no provider.
- No official MemoryAgentBench or EvoMemBench bytes. Reject protected,
  private, held-out, and upstream bytes fail-closed.

### Oracle

- Path: `eval/public/wmbs_m06.py`. Standard library only. No new dependency.
- Contract surface: universal `ingest`, `retrieve`, and `answer` across
  episodes. No private consolidation API and no private no-consolidation
  control. No privileged internal signal.
- When a public no-consolidation control cannot be exercised through a public
  control, use the universal no-memory control and disclose the missing
  ablation. Do not invent a private control to avoid that disclosure.
- Metrics: utility delta versus no-consolidation/no-memory, harmful promotion,
  compounding-error rate, cross-episode transfer, storage, and cost.
- Intervals are descriptive and finite-corpus only.
- Latency, tokens, and calls stay `unsupported`.
- No baseline-improvement field and no non-inferiority field.
- Storage and cost are descriptive observations. This cell admits no provider
  budget. A missing provider spend is explicit `unsupported`, not a billed
  zero.
- Tests pin the arithmetic with hand-calculated cases before the oracle
  exists. Those development definitions are not the spec acceptance test.
