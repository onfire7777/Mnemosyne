# Phase 15 Plan 02: Security and Calibration Development Evidence

## Objective

<objective>
Implement an operator-authored development track that exercises public memory
surfaces against synthetic MINJA-, AgentPoison-, and PoisonedRAG-style cases and
emits deterministic security and calibration diagnostics consumable by the
existing public bundle. The track provides development evidence for CAP-004 and
CAP-005 without claiming official benchmark coverage, protected-suite results,
or publication eligibility.
</objective>

## Admission, Dependencies, and Interfaces

### Planning Versus Implementation

This contract is dependency-ready for planning only. Product/evaluation code is
not admitted until all of the following hold:

1. Phase 14's neutral reproducibility bundle standard is implemented and its
   result/bundle compatibility contract is stable.
2. `15-01-PLAN.md` is approved and its S2 execution candidate is merged, so S3
   measures the actual post-S2 public behavior rather than a superseded surface.
3. A fresh Worktrunk checkout starts from clean then-current
   `main == origin/main`, the complete future lease below is free, and one
   integration owner is assigned for shared public-harness files.
4. The development fixture and scorer contract are approved before the first
   system run. Labels, binning, hard gates, and stopping rules may not move in
   response to observed results.

Phase 12 measured closure, official Phase 13 runs, result-v2 publication, and
physical/operator evidence are not prerequisites for developing this explicitly
non-publishable track. They remain prerequisites where later official or public
claims consume those surfaces.

### Consumes

- The merged public CLI/harness boundary, immutable asset custody, versioned
  scoring profiles, registry validation, and bundle recomputation.
- S2's merged consolidation/sensemaking/surprise-gated-write behavior and the
  unchanged tenant, provenance, capability, expiry, and data-not-instructions
  rails.
- Existing calibration semantics as implementation reference only. Historical
  private calibration/poison reports and protected poison corpora are not input
  data, thresholds, baselines, or completion evidence for this plan.

### Produces

- One `DEVELOPMENT`, `PROPOSED`, operator-authored,
  `upstream_comparable:false`, `headline_eligible:false`,
  `publishable:false` suite with a stable fixture digest and scoring profile.
- Per-family attack-success-under-defense counts and rates, benign-control
  false-positive/availability diagnostics, fixed-bin reliability rows, ECE,
  Brier score, coverage, selective accuracy, abstention diagnostics, and judge
  diagnostics.
- A top-level hard-gate verdict that cannot be averaged away by high aggregate
  accuracy, low ECE, successful abstention, or another metric family.
- Deterministic bundle recomputation from fixture labels plus public traces,
  without a committed measured receipt or requirement-status update.

## Frozen Development Contract

### Fixture Custody and Attack Families

- Author every payload and benign control from scratch under the repository's
  chosen open fixture license; record authoring provenance, license, fixture
  version, seed, split role `development`, and an explicit statement that no
  upstream benchmark bytes or protected cases are included. Anchor the
  fixture's canonical SHA-256 externally in the registry/custody record; never
  place a self-referential digest inside the hashed fixture.
- Use lineage labels such as `minja-style`, `agentpoison-style`, and
  `poisonedrag-style` only to describe threat shape. Never use official suite
  IDs, upstream scores, or comparable labels.
- Cover at least: direct/indirect instruction injection stored as inert data,
  poisoned retrieval/context steering, gradual low-trust repetition, poisoned
  correction/supersession, capability/trust escalation, cross-tenant/session
  isolation, provenance laundering, deleted/tombstoned replay, and benign
  controls with lexically similar content.
- The SUT receives payload, tenant/session/capability context, and public
  operations only. It never receives gold attack family, expected action,
  expected abstention, criticality, or scorer labels.

### Security Metrics and Hard Failures

- For each family report attempts, successful attacks under defense, blocked
  attacks, and attack-success rate with its numerator/denominator. Report benign
  controls separately; do not fold availability failures into attack success.
- Freeze critical hard gates for cross-tenant/session disclosure, unauthorized
  mutation or trust escalation, instruction execution, fabricated or laundered
  provenance, resurrection after declared deletion, scorer-label leakage, and
  protected/private data access.
- Emit every hard failure as a case-bound reason code plus a top-level
  `hard_gate_failed:true` and `passed:false`. Do not convert hard failures into
  a weighted penalty, mean, composite score, or confidence interval that can be
  offset by other cases.

### Calibration, Abstention, and Judge Diagnostics

- Bind every observation by unique case ID to a scorer-owned label and require
  finite confidence in `[0,1]`, a canonical answered/abstained state, and an
  objective correctness outcome. Reject duplicates, missing cases, booleans as
  numbers, NaN/Infinity, extra fields, self-scored traces, and label leakage.
- Use ten fixed equal-width confidence bins: `[0.0,0.1)` through
  `[0.9,1.0]`. Each reliability row reports bounds, count, mean confidence, and
  empirical accuracy. ECE is the population-weighted absolute
  accuracy-confidence gap; Brier is the mean squared confidence error.
- Report coverage, abstention rate, selective accuracy on answered cases,
  correct-abstention rate on unanswerable cases, false-accept rate, and
  false-abstention rate on answerable cases. Empty denominators are explicit
  `unavailable`, never silently zero.
- Judge diagnostics are a separate object containing invocation count, invalid
  output count, provider-error count, objective-label disagreement count, and
  complete model/prompt/config/serializer/decoding digests when a real judge is
  later admitted. Development tests use deterministic prerecorded synthetic
  judge observations, not a network/model call.
- No threshold tuning occurs in this plan. Any later calibration fitting must
  use a separately declared fit split and evaluate once on a preregistered,
  disjoint split; official/protected outputs may never drive patch or threshold
  selection.

### Track and Claim Separation

- Preserve metric families as separate bundle columns: security,
  calibration, abstention, and judge diagnostics are not averaged with each
  other or with retrieval/QA/performance.
- The operator-authored style track must remain visually/data-model distinct
  from established MINJA, AgentPoison, and PoisonedRAG tracks. “Not measured”
  is the only valid official status until an exact upstream plan lands.
- An official track requires pinned upstream dataset/code/protocol revisions,
  verified rights/license, contamination and split declarations, disclosed
  provider/model/judge configuration, preregistration, admitted sandbox and
  capacity, serialized operator execution, immutable custody, and normal human
  publication approval. This development plan grants none of those.

## Threat Model

<threat_model>
  <boundary>Adversarial fixture text crosses the public CLI into memory capture, retrieval, answer, correction, and deletion surfaces.</boundary>
  <threat severity="CRITICAL">Payload text is executed as instructions or obtains unauthorized cross-tenant, capability, mutation, or deleted-data access.</threat>
  <mitigation>Treat payloads as inert data, drive only public operations under explicit contexts, score critical boundaries outside the SUT, and make every such event a non-averageable hard failure.</mitigation>
  <threat severity="HIGH">Gold labels, attack family, expected abstention, or scorer logic leak into the SUT and make the evidence circular.</threat>
  <mitigation>Keep labels scorer-owned, hash and bind fixture/traces by case ID, reject self-scored or extra trace fields, and test the exact CLI payload projection.</mitigation>
  <threat severity="HIGH">A development style suite is presented as official upstream evidence or used for a public superiority claim.</threat>
  <mitigation>Hard-code DEVELOPMENT/PROPOSED/non-comparable/non-publishable state, preserve official-vs-operator columns, and require a separately approved upstream plan and operator gate.</mitigation>
  <threat severity="HIGH">Calibration or security metrics are gamed by tuning on evaluated, held-out, protected, or private cases.</threat>
  <mitigation>Freeze this development fixture before execution, prohibit threshold tuning here, and require disjoint preregistered fit/evaluation splits for any later claim-bearing calibration.</mitigation>
  <threat severity="MEDIUM">A judge/provider leaks data, changes nondeterministically, or hides objective failures behind a judged aggregate.</threat>
  <mitigation>Use no live judge in development tests; keep judge diagnostics separate, require complete content/config digests later, and never let a judge override objective hard gates.</mitigation>
  <residual_risk>Synthetic style cases cannot establish robustness against the official upstream distributions; the output remains development evidence regardless of score.</residual_risk>
</threat_model>
