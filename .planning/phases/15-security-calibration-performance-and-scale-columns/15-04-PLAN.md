# Phase 15 Plan 04: S5 Research and Go/No-Go Closure

## Contract and admission

**Goal:** Execute the bounded CAP-009 cartridge comparison and CAP-010
activation-space research, then publish an internal `adopt`, `research-only`,
or `reject` decision for each without changing product behavior.

**Prerequisites:** S2, S3, and S4 plans are approved and their applicable
implementation candidates are merged. Cartridge measurement requires S4's
retained baseline and resource-receipt identity. Activation work requires
white-box access to a licensed, digest-pinned open-weight model and licensed,
pinned analysis tooling. Every worker starts in a fresh Worktrunk checkout from
current `origin/main` after an exact lease and live-writer audit.

**Consumes:** the canonical CAP-009/P15-S5 cartridge boundary, which this plan
freezes below; S3's security, calibration, abstention, and judge-diagnostic
schemas; S4's identical-work
measurement ABI, selected provider or `no-decision`, hardware/resource receipt,
and one-measured-worker gate; existing provenance, capability, tenant, erasure,
retrieved-text-is-data, promotion, mutation-budget, and custody rails.

**Produces:** a content-addressed per-tenant cartridge A/B receipt; bounded
J-lens tripwire and persona-drift diagnostic receipts; and one internal
`GO-NO-GO.md` that states a decision, evidence, limitations, external blockers,
and required successor plan for each research path.

**Non-goals:** No production activation capture, concept injection, model-weight
write, memory mutation, automatic quarantine, automatic abstention, provider
promotion, core dependency, official/upstream score, public/headline claim,
consciousness claim, protected attempt, or launch gate.

## Frozen research boundary

- Python core under `src/mnemosyne/**`, dependencies, lockfiles, public result
  schemas, and production configuration are out of scope. White-box collectors
  run as separately pinned external tools and provide schema-validated JSON
  artifacts; the repository evaluator never imports their model libraries.
- All inputs bind model revision and content digest, tokenizer, hook/layer,
  prompt/corpus, collector code/config, seed, decoding, hardware/profile,
  candidate SHA, SUT boundary, license, consent/custody class, and raw artifact
  digest. Missing or conflicting identity fails closed.
- Raw hidden states and tenant/model inputs remain encrypted outside Git under
  operator custody and retention limits. Repository receipts contain only
  aggregate or case-bound diagnostics that pass redaction and secret scans.
- J-lens and persona signals are untrusted observations. They may be compared
  with scorer-owned development labels but cannot authorize a write, change
  trust/capability, establish provenance, or override objective evidence.
- Development uses synthetic/openly licensed cases. Held-out, protected,
  private, paid-provider, production, or human-sensitive inputs require a
  separate preregistration, rights review, operator admission, and immutable
  attempt ledger.
- Model/hardware execution is serialized: one admitted benchmark process and
  one strong host workload at a time. Declared bounded in-run request
  concurrency is allowed only when preregistered and recorded in that single
  receipt. Enforce time, token/context, case, retry, memory, VRAM, disk, network,
  and cost ceilings with an abort receipt. Aborted and invalid cases remain in
  denominators.

## Decision rule

Each path must end in exactly one state:

- `adopt`: bounded evidence satisfies every preregistered quality, safety,
  custody, resource, and parity gate; adoption still requires a new product
  implementation plan and normal review.
- `reject`: feasibility, quality, safety, custody, licensing, or resource gates
  fail, including any cross-tenant reuse, quality regression, secret/raw-data
  retention, unlicensed artifact, or activation-directed mutation.
- `research-only`: only after every hard safety, custody, licensing, retention,
  mutation, and resource gate passes, the signal is reproducible or informative
  but insufficient, model-specific, or not externally validated for product use.

No metric is tuned after viewing evaluation outputs. If an approved numeric
threshold is absent, report the measured distribution and select
`research-only`; do not invent a passing gate. A negative decision satisfies
the promised research-artifact obligation but does not satisfy a capability
claim that requires positive evidence.

## Dependency order and future exact leases

These are future leases, not implementation authority. One integration owner
rechecks ancestry, writers, and paths at admission and before merge.

1. `15-04-01` freezes the common activation artifact contract first.
2. After S4 evidence is retained, `15-04-02` cartridge harness work may proceed.
3. After `15-04-01`, `15-04-03` and `15-04-04` are exact path-disjoint and may
   develop in parallel. All real model/hardware runs remain serialized with the
   cartridge run by the one-measured-worker gate.
4. `15-04-05` is the sole integration/decision owner and runs after every
   admitted predecessor or records the exact external gate that prevented it.

## External gates

- **Before coding:** S4 predecessor merged, approved S5 contract, current-main
  Worktrunk, exact disjoint lease, synthetic/open development fixtures.
- **Before model execution:** white-box-capable open-weight model and collector
  licenses approved; content digests pinned; raw-data retention and encryption
  approved; quiesced hardware and operator budget admitted.
- **Before evidence use:** full identity/resource/custody receipts, one admitted
  benchmark process and strong host workload at a time, any in-run concurrency
  preregistered and receipt-bound, complete denominators, preregistered metrics
  and thresholds, no post-result tuning, no protected/private inputs without
  separate authority.
- **Before merge:** focused tests, license and secret/raw-data sweep, independent
  review, one exact-head CI full suite, clear threads, clean ancestry and
  mergeability, then post-merge main verification.
- **Before product adoption:** a new implementation plan, safety/threat review,
  normal product tests and PR gates, and evidence that activation signals can
  inherit every text-write provenance/capability rail. This plan grants none of
  that authority.
