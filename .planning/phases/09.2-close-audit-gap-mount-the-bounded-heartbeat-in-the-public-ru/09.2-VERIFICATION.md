## Blocking Findings

### HIGH — release evidence accepts a terminal restart contradiction

`_release_worker_run_evidence_findings` accepts a cycle-1 terminal hard stop
followed by a healthy cycle 2 and healthy final report. The independent
mutation changed cycle 1 to:

- `ok=false`
- `lifecycle=unhealthy`
- `failure_code=workspace_heartbeat_hard_stop`
- `heartbeat_safety.hard_stop=true`

while leaving the later healthy tick and final evidence intact. The validator
returned `[]`. This violates the terminal freeze/no-in-process-restart contract
in D-06, D-13, D-16 and the plan must-have requiring rejection of internally
inconsistent heartbeat claims.

Required fix: once any cycle is terminal/unhealthy, reject any later increment
in tick/attempt counts, healthy lifecycle, or healthy final report; require the
final report to preserve the terminal failure state.

### HIGH — release evidence accepts unbounded attempt counts

Setting `attempted_ticks=999999` on both cycle projections and the final
projection also returned `[]`. The validator currently checks only that the
field is a nonnegative integer; it does not bound it, relate it to cycle count,
relate it to `tick_count`, or enforce monotonic one-attempt-per-live-cycle
semantics. This violates D-04, D-05, D-13 and the explicit must-have that
release evidence reject unbounded or internally inconsistent claims.

Required fix: enforce `attempted_ticks <= worker summary cycles`, monotonicity,
one increment per non-frozen cycle, consistency with `tick_count`, and the
configured controller bound.
